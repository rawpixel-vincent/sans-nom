# Phase 3 Exit Criteria — v1.0 Launch

v1.0 ships only when all of the following hold.

## 1. Monetization model selected and load-tested

The team has chosen one of [the monetization options](../../project/cost/monetization-options.md) (BYOK, subscription, or single-purchase + capped runs) and verified it works at the projected v1.0 scale. "Verified" means the payment / key-handoff flow is implemented, tested with real transactions in a closed cohort, and operationally supportable.

## 2. Chest-crest mechanic prose-verified against canon

A sample of 20 chest-crest scenes (Sans-Nom hearing each of the 8 canonical ancestors at least twice across the sample) is reviewed by the narrative designer. Pass: ≥18 of 20 are graded "in tone" — the ancestor speaks in their voice, the witness-bound logic holds, the moment lands with the IP's weight.

## 3. Long-arc continuity holds

Five completed playthroughs spanning 20+ hours and 5+ sessions each are reviewed for end-to-end continuity. Pass: no playthrough exhibits character-amnesia events, contradictory representations, or session-boundary glitches that break immersion.

## 4. Audio narration quality is acceptable

If TTS shipped: opt-in adoption is meaningful (≥15% of players turn it on at least once); per-character voice consistency is graded ≥80% across a sample of 30 audio scenes; cost per audio scene is bounded and projected.

If TTS did not ship (cut from Phase 3 due to quality or budget): document the cut and ship without it. Audio is a stretch goal, not a launch blocker.

## 5. Cost-per-turn within the v1.0 monetization envelope

Whatever monetization model was chosen, the cost-per-turn budget supports it. For BYOK: the player carries the cost; the team's verification is that BYOK key handoff works smoothly. For subscription: per-tier cost falls within the subscription's budget at the targeted player population. For single-purchase: cost-per-credit is bounded and the team's profit margin is real.

## 6. Bushitaka decision arc reads in tone across player paths

The Bushitaka decision can land in many shapes: Sans-Nom performs the kill (canonical), refuses (Aghora-style), dies before the choice, walks away in some other manner. Each shape must read as canonical-feeling.

Sample: 10 playthroughs that reach the Bushitaka decision arc. Pass: each ending is graded "in tone" by the narrative designer. No ending feels like a generic-game ending.

## 7. ADRs closed

Any new ADRs from Phase 3 are status `accepted`. The decisions log has no `proposed` items at launch.

## What it means to fail

v1.0 does not launch. The team identifies the failed proof, scopes remediation, re-evaluates at the next monthly review. If multiple proofs fail, escalate to a project-level decision (delay launch, cut scope, or accept gaps with documented mitigations).

## Post-v1.0

Phase 3 is the last specified phase. v2.0 planning happens after v1.0 ships and gathers data. Likely v2.0 directions: family-tree views, multiplayer Sans-Noms, additional IP adaptations using the same engine. None of these are committed to here.
