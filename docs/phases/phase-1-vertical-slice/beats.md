# Phase 1 Major Beats

The available narrative beats in the vertical slice. None is forced. The Narrator selects which to land based on player evolution + scene context.

This file is the canonical list. The [slice-spec](slice-spec.md) summarizes them; this file goes deeper for each.

## Beat A — First Tonto story

**Trigger.** The player invokes [Ask Tonto](../../mechanics/verbs/ask-tonto.md) for the first time, OR Tonto interrupts a quiet moment to begin a story unbidden (Narrator's call).

**Stakes.** Establishes the Tonto-narration cadence — long sentences, robot-narrator register, generative recall over Tonto's witness-bound representation of canon. If players don't engage with this beat, the [Ask Tonto verb](../../mechanics/verbs/ask-tonto.md) loses its place in the loop.

**Variation.** The first story might be about Othon, about Honorata, about Sans-Nom's name, about Aghora's childhood — whatever Tonto judges relevant to what the player just did. The Narrator decides.

## Beat B — Aghora notices the wrong questions

**Trigger.** Sans-Nom has asked Aghora 3+ questions about subjects she has been deflecting (his father, the eastern hall, his name). The Narrator stages the turn.

**Stakes.** A turning point in their relationship. Aghora's canonical response is withdrawal — not anger, not explanation, just distance. Sans-Nom learns something about the limits of asking.

**Variation.** What specifically Aghora does or says depends on her [actor-card](../../mechanics/cast/aghora.md) state. She might silence him. She might leave the room. She might sit with him in silence longer than is comfortable. All are in-character.

## Beat C — First Bushitaka stance lesson

**Trigger.** Aghora decides Sans-Nom is ready. Threshold: typically a quiet morning after he has demonstrated patience, OR after a moment where his energy needs channeling.

**Stakes.** The form is introduced. Sans-Nom learns the bodily discipline. He does not yet know what the form is *for*. The Narrator must not over-explain.

**Variation.** Earth stance comes naturally; Sky is harder. The exact stances and the metaphors used depend on canon retrieval at the moment.

## Beat D — The eastern hall's lock breaks

**Trigger.** Narrator-decided, typically once Sans-Nom is old enough to be alone in the compound. The lock breaks for reasons that are not obvious — accident, age of the mechanism, some force the player's input has stirred.

**Stakes.** Sans-Nom either looks or doesn't. The Narrator does not nudge. If he looks, his card updates with a stable trait about the stasis pod. If not, the moment closes — Aghora discovers the broken lock, replaces it, says nothing.

**Variation.** What's in the eastern hall is *not* fully revealed in this slice. Sans-Nom sees enough to know it's a person sealed in stasis. He does not see enough to know it's his grandfather. That revelation is later.

## Beat E — Aghora tells Sans-Nom his name (or doesn't)

**Trigger.** Available throughout the slice. Lands when Aghora's actor-card state and the player's accumulated questions justify it.

**Stakes.** The naming moment is one of the IP's central rituals — *Sans-Nom* itself is a name about not having a name. Whatever Aghora says (or refuses to say), the moment should land with weight.

**Variation.** Some playthroughs end the slice without this. Some have it quietly. Some have it as an argument. All are valid.

## Beat F — A Vicenta recording is found

**Trigger.** Optional Narrator beat. Lands rarely (~1 in 4 playthroughs, judgment call).

**Stakes.** The first time the player meets a dead character through unmediated source material. Tonto context-frames the moment.

**Variation.** What the recording contains is generated by Vicenta's actor at scene time, treating the recording's date and context as the prompt. Different playthroughs surface different recordings.

## Cross-beat principles

- No beat is required. A complete playthrough may land 6 of 6 or 0 of 6.
- Beats can interleave. Beat B (Aghora notices) might happen before, during, or after Beat C (first stance lesson).
- The Narrator is the only entity that decides beat triggering. There is no scripted timer.
- Beats that *don't* land in this playthrough may land in a future Sans-Nom's playthrough — see [Pillar 4](../../principles/pillars/04-replay-is-medium.md).
