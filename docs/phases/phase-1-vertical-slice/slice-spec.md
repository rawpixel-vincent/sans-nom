# Phase 1 Slice Spec — Childhood at Aghora's Compound

The complete specification of the vertical slice. Use this to evaluate whether the build is on-spec.

## Setting

Aghora's compound on a remote moon. Stone, gardens, a single forbidden hallway. The compound is materially specific — it has rooms, weather, smells. The Narrator should give the player a sense of place that does not require a level designer.

## Cast

### Active

- [Aghora](../../mechanics/cast/aghora.md) — Sans-Nom's parent. Decides what he is told and what is hidden. The compound is hers.
- [Tonto](../../mechanics/cast/tonto.md) — ambient presence and lore-conduit. In his alcove most of the time; can be summoned via [Ask Tonto](../../mechanics/verbs/ask-tonto.md).
- [Lothar](../../mechanics/cast/lothar.md) — small ambient companion. Used sparingly.
- Sans-Nom — the player.

### Referenced (Tonto-narrated only in this slice)

- [Othon](../../mechanics/cast/othon.md), [Honorata](../../mechanics/cast/honorata.md), [Aghnar](../../mechanics/cast/aghnar.md), [Oda](../../mechanics/cast/oda.md), [Steelhead](../../mechanics/cast/steelhead.md), [Vicenta](../../mechanics/cast/vicenta.md) — through Tonto's stories only. **Exception:** Vicenta may be encountered through a recording (see "Major beats" below).

### Generated NPCs

The Narrator may invent: a servant in the kitchen, a visiting trader, a passing soldier, a child Sans-Nom briefly encounters. See [generated-npcs](../../mechanics/cast/generated-npcs.md). Cap on simultaneously-active generated cast in this slice: ~5 across a full playthrough.

## Time span

Sans-Nom ages 6 through 12. Approximately ~80 plausible scenes per playthrough, but variance is wide — some players may produce 40, others 200.

## Locations within the slice

- **The courtyard** — gathering place; where Aghora and Sans-Nom often speak.
- **The kitchen** — domestic scenes; where Tonto and Lothar are sometimes present.
- **Aghora's training room** — once Aghora has decided Sans-Nom is ready. Bushitaka stances begin here.
- **Tonto's alcove** — Tonto's home base; where most Tonto-storytelling scenes resolve.
- **The gardens** — a place of relative peace; can be a setting for Sans-Nom's reflections.
- **The eastern hall (forbidden)** — leads to Steelhead's stasis pod. Accessible only by player initiative; the lock breaks once during the slice (Narrator-decided when).

## Major beats the Narrator may or may not introduce

These are *available* beats. None is forced. The Narrator decides which land in any given playthrough, in what order, with what weight.

- **First Tonto story.** Player-triggered (the player asks Tonto a question). Establishes the Tonto-narration cadence. Phase-1 success criterion: players seek out subsequent Tonto stories on their own.
- **The day Aghora notices Sans-Nom is asking the wrong questions.** A turning point in their relationship. Aghora's response is canonical — withdrawal, not anger.
- **First Bushitaka stance lesson.** The form is introduced. Earth comes naturally; Sky is harder. Sans-Nom does not yet know what the form is *for*.
- **The eastern hall's lock breaks once.** Sans-Nom either looks or doesn't. The Narrator does not nudge. If Sans-Nom looks, his card updates with a stable trait about the stasis pod. If not, Aghora discovers the broken lock and the moment opens differently.
- **Aghora tells Sans-Nom his name (or doesn't).** Some playthroughs end the slice without this. Others have it as a quiet moment. Others have it as an argument. Whichever path lands, it should feel earned by what Sans-Nom has become.
- **A Vicenta recording is found.** Optional Narrator beat (not in every playthrough). The first time the player meets a dead character through unmediated source material. Tonto context-frames the moment.

## What the Narrator must avoid in this slice

- Any introduction of the Bushitaka decision (Sans-Nom is too young; the gravity is felt, not explained).
- Death of Sans-Nom (death-state is Phase-2 only).
- Travel beyond the compound (one location, one session).
- Any IP-violating event (see [ip-commitments](../../mechanics/world/ip-commitments.md)).
