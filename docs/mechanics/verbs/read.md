# Verb: Read

The default state. Consume the Narrator's prose.

## Trigger

Implicit — the player is always reading except when actively choosing or typing. The UI's primary affordance is the prose pane; the choice list and input field sit below it.

## Behavior

The Narrator outputs prose, scene by scene. Scenes range from two paragraphs to two pages depending on what the moment deserves. The Narrator self-paces — short scenes for quick beats, long scenes for emotional weight.

## Notes for the Narrator

- A "scene" is whatever feels like a single moment. A breakfast that becomes a confrontation can be one scene or three; the Narrator decides.
- Italic interiority (the "little italic voice" the player remembers from the [player fantasy](../../principles/player-fantasy.md)) is the Narrator's tool for surfacing what a character is *thinking* without violating witness-bound representations — only Sans-Nom's interiority is shown, plus narrator-as-omniscient observations marked typographically.
- The default font and pacing matter. See [frontend phase notes](../../phases/phase-1-vertical-slice/scope.md).
