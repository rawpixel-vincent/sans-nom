# Dead and Absent Characters

Most of the dynasty is dead before Sans-Nom is born. They participate in the game without being live actors in scenes.

## The four channels of absent participation

### 1. Tonto's stories

Tonto is the dynasty's robotic narrator and the lore-conduit. When Tonto tells a story about Edna, Tonto's actor agent generates the story drawing on Tonto's witness-bound representation of Edna. The player learns about Edna through Tonto's eyes specifically. See [tonto.md](tonto.md) and the [Ask Tonto verb](../verbs/ask-tonto.md).

If the player later finds an unmediated source (a recording of Edna directly), their representation of Edna diverges from Tonto's. This is exactly what Honcho's directional representations are for.

### 2. Memory fragments

Recordings, holograms, sealed rooms with old presences. The Narrator can introduce an artifact and run a scene around it — the player views a holographic Honorata addressing a long-dead audience; the player listens to a recording of Vicenta laughing.

The artifact's content is generated by the absent character's actor agent at scene time, treating the artifact's date and context as the prompt. This means the same artifact can read slightly differently across playthroughs, depending on how the Narrator framed the encounter.

### 3. The chest-crest (Phase 3)

Sans-Nom inherits the crest in late game. The crest enables the dead to speak through it — meaning ancestor peers' representations of Sans-Nom become readable to Sans-Nom directly. This is the Phase-3 mechanic that operationalizes the original GDD's pillar *Inheritance is mechanical, not narrative*. See [tonal fidelity](../../principles/tonal-fidelity.md).

Implementation: Sans-Nom's actor agent is given access to the named ancestor's directional card-for-Sans-Nom; the resulting prose is framed as the ancestor speaking *to* Sans-Nom, through the crest, in their own voice.

### 4. Apparitions (psychic / spiritual)

The Narrator may invoke this for [Aghnar](aghnar.md) specifically, given his canonical psychic legacy. The mechanic is similar to the chest-crest but framed as an unbidden apparition — Aghnar appears, says what he says, leaves. The Narrator decides when this is in tone.

## Why this works architecturally

An absent character is still a peer in Honcho. Their card seed is loaded; their representations of others are queryable; their actor agent can be invoked with the prompt "you are responding through Tonto's recollection" or "you are speaking through the chest-crest." The architecture does not distinguish between living and dead at the storage layer — only the Narrator decides when an absent character can be summoned and through which channel.

This matches the IP's grammar perfectly. The dead live in the architecture. See [Pillar 2](../../principles/pillars/02-characters-witness-bound.md).
