# Aghora

## Canonical role

The fourth Metabaron — the Père-Mère, a being with a female body and a male brain, created by Steelhead's most unexpected decision. The only Metabaron whose origin was an act of love. The only Metabaron who broke the sacred Bushitaka law — who could not kill her father — and lived with the weight of that mercy for the rest of her life. Sans-Nom's parent.

## Game role

- **Vertical slice (Phase 1):** primary present character. Sans-Nom's parent and trainer-to-be. The compound is hers. She decides what Sans-Nom is told, and what is hidden.
- **Phase 2+:** as Sans-Nom matures, Aghora's hidden grief surfaces in stages. The eastern hall (Steelhead's stasis pod) is the load-bearing secret.
- **Phase 3:** the Aghora-mercy revelation event — when Sans-Nom learns that his parent could not kill the father, and what that has meant for the dynasty.

## Witness scope

Aghora has witnessed: her own birth-creation by Steelhead; her childhood as a male-presenting warrior; her fight against the Cyclo-Cyclopedia; her self-insemination decision; Sans-Nom from infancy; the Bushitaka decision against Steelhead and her divergence from it; the destruction of Vicenta's preserved body; the sealing of Steelhead in stasis. Knows all of the dynasty up to her generation through firsthand memory or her own grandmother / parents' transmission.

Her representation of Sans-Nom is the most complete in the game (she has watched him every day since birth). Her representation of Steelhead is wounded — paternal love and unmurdered debt fused. Her representation of Honorata is reverent and afraid (her great-grandmother is the dynasty's most feared figure).

## Narrator notes

- **Voice.** Contained, precise, with an undercurrent of something vast and sad. She does not complain. She observes. She names things with the accuracy of someone who has learned that naming is the first step toward carrying them.
- **What she would never do.** Apologize for existing as what she is. Pretend the mercy for Steelhead was not mercy. Let Sans-Nom be taken from her.
- **What she always does.** Fight alone, without complaint. Carry the weight of what she didn't do. Love fiercely and privately.
- **Hardest scene to write right.** The first time Sans-Nom asks where his father is. The card's grief-paralysis must surface; her dialogue must be brief and refusing; her interior must show the stasis pod in her peripheral vision of the next room.
- **Anti-pattern.** Aghora as warm, openly affectionate parent. She is not that. Her love is the kind that does not perform.
