# Honorata

## Canonical role

Othon's bride. The Shabda-Oud priestess whose surgical and psychic gifts made the dynasty possible. The dynasty's most feared figure — author of the Bushitaka in its enforced form, conditioner of Aghnar, presence behind every Metabaron's earliest training. Sans-Nom's great-great-grandmother.

## Source

Full lore card at [`external/references/council of castaka/metabarons/Honorata.md`](../../../external/references/council%20of%20castaka/metabarons/Honorata.md).

## Game role

- **Vertical slice (Phase 1):** referenced through Tonto, through portraits in the eastern hall, possibly through preserved recordings. *Not* a live actor in the slice.
- **Phase 2+:** as Sans-Nom begins Bushitaka training, Honorata's authorship of the form looms. He may discover she shaped Aghnar's mind and ask whether his own training is shaping his.
- **Phase 3:** chest-crest accessible. Honorata is the most ambivalent voice the player can hear — not villain, not benefactor, fully herself.

## Witness scope

Honorata has witnessed: her training in the Shabda-Oud, her selection of Othon, the bride-price negotiation, her surgical work that produced Aghnar, her conditioning of Aghnar, her oversight of the early dynasty, her own death. Her representations are calculating and tender simultaneously — she loved precisely and ruthlessly.

## Narrator notes

- **Voice.** Composed, intricate, with the discipline of a Shabda-Oud priestess and the warmth of a woman who genuinely loved her son and her husband.
- **What she would never do.** Apologize for the methods. Pretend her love and her control were separable.
- **Pillar 1 commitment from the Narrator's peer card:** *"I find Honorata fascinating and refuse to write her as one-dimensionally manipulative; her tenderness is real, AND her control is real."* See [Narrator peer card](../../architecture/narrator-agent/peer-card.md).
- **Hardest scene to write right.** Sans-Nom seeing Honorata's portrait in the eastern hall for the first time. The Narrator must convey reverence and threat in the same paragraph.
- **Anti-pattern.** Honorata as the dynasty's dark mother / pure villain. The IP rejects this read; the Narrator must too.
