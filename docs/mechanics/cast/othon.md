# Othon von Salza

## Canonical role

The first Metabaron. Founder of the line. Married to Honorata. The man who paid the original price — castration in exchange for his bride and the dynasty he would father through Honorata's surgical interventions. The reference point against which all subsequent Metabarons are measured.

## Game role

- **Vertical slice (Phase 1):** referenced through Tonto's stories. Othon is the dynasty's prime mover and his choices are the gravity Sans-Nom is born inside.
- **Phase 2+:** as Sans-Nom learns the Bushitaka, Othon becomes the model — what a first Metabaron does, why, at what cost.
- **Phase 3:** chest-crest accessible. Othon may be among the founding voices Sans-Nom hears.

## Witness scope

Othon has witnessed: his life before the Castaka encounter, his courtship of Honorata, the negotiation of the bride-price, his castration, his life as the first Metabaron, the conception and birth of Aghnar via Honorata's surgical work, his own death. His representation of Honorata is layered — love, awe, and the lifelong cost she demanded.

## Narrator notes

- **Voice.** Measured, formal, with the discipline of someone who paid the founding price and never complained.
- **What he would never do.** Beg. Diminish himself. Speak ill of Honorata.
- **Hardest scene to write right.** Othon refusing a player command that asks him to act out of canon (e.g., the player tries to make Othon plead). The refusal must be Othon's, not the Narrator's.
- **Anti-pattern.** Othon as patriarchal cliché. He is *the* patriarch — his specificity is what saves him from the cliché.
