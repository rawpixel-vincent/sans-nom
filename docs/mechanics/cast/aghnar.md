# Aghnar von Salza

## Canonical role

The second Metabaron. Son of Othon and Honorata — a child whose conception was paid for in a castration vow and whose mother's psychic conditioning shaped him before he could choose otherwise. The Metabaron with the strongest psychic legacy.

Full lore card at [`external/references/council of castaka/metabarons/AghnarVonSalza.md`](../../../external/references/council%20of%20castaka/metabarons/AghnarVonSalza.md).

## Game role

- **Vertical slice (Phase 1):** referenced through Tonto's stories.
- **Phase 2+:** Aghnar's psychic legacy is foreshadowed as the dynasty's hidden inheritance line. Sans-Nom may discover that whatever he is becoming, some of it descends from Aghnar's mind.
- **Phase 3:** Aghnar specifically is the Metabaron the Narrator can choose to invoke through *apparition* — psychic / spiritual presence rather than recording or chest-crest.

## Witness scope

Aghnar has witnessed: his own conception's costs (told to him retrospectively), his mother's conditioning, his training under Othon, his marriage, his fatherhood of Steelhead, his own death. His psychic legacy means his card includes representations of people he never bodily met — a load-bearing exception to the witness-bound rule, justified by canon. Phase-3 chest-crest mechanics may make this matter.

## Narrator notes

- **Voice.** Quiet, watchful, with the wariness of someone who knows his own mind was shaped before he could consent.
- **What he would never do.** Pretend Honorata was kind to him.
- **Hardest scene to write right.** Aghnar appearing as apparition to Sans-Nom in Phase 3. The line between psychic presence and the player's projection must stay deliberately blurred.
- **Anti-pattern.** Aghnar as straightforwardly noble. He is shaped, and he knows it.
