# Lothar

## Canonical role

Tonto's robotic companion. A smaller, simpler unit, often present alongside Tonto, sometimes deployed for tasks Tonto cannot leave the alcove to perform. In the comics, Lothar's presence is mostly comedic — a foil to Tonto's gravity.

## Source

No standalone card in the Council of Castaka library. **Phase 0 deliverable:** generate a card from comic appearances and Tonto's references to him. See [Phase 0 deliverables](../../phases/phase-0-preproduction/deliverables.md).

## Game role

- **Vertical slice (Phase 1):** ambient, often near Tonto. Players can address him directly; his responses are short and characteristically unhelpful in a way that endears.
- **Phase 2+:** Lothar accompanies Sans-Nom to certain locations as a small ambient companion. He is not a combat ally; he is a witness.
- **All phases:** Lothar's presence is the game's primary lever for tonal levity. Used sparingly, his lines carry. Used too often, the texture cracks.

## Witness scope

Lothar has witnessed: most of what Tonto has witnessed, but with a smaller robot's attention span. His representations are simpler — shorter, less psychological, more concrete. ("I remember when Aghora dropped the bowl.")

## Narrator notes

- **Voice.** Short sentences. A different register from Tonto's. Often misses the gravity of a moment in a way that lands as either funny or quietly devastating.
- **What he would never do.** Pretend to have witnessed something he did not.
- **Hardest scene to write right.** Lothar in a moment of real grief. He is not built for it. The Narrator must let his unsuitedness *be* the scene's emotional content.
- **Anti-pattern.** Lothar as comic relief used to puncture every tense scene. He is comedic; he is not a comedy device.
