# Doña Vicenta Gabriela de Rokha

## Canonical role

Steelhead's beloved. The Berard noblewoman whose death broke him. Killed by an assassin's projectile meant for Steelhead. Her preserved body became the object of Steelhead's grief and the trigger for his Melmoth descent. Aghora's loved-but-never-met grandmother.

## Game role

- **Vertical slice (Phase 1):** referenced only — through Tonto stories, through Aghora's silences. Probably introduced via a recording (the GDD calls this out as the canon's most poignant single-character introduction).
- **Phase 2+:** as Sans-Nom's understanding of the dynasty deepens, Vicenta becomes the example case for the IP's grammar — *what one death does to the people who survive it*.
- **Phase 3:** if Sans-Nom inherits the chest-crest, Vicenta can be among the voices he hears. Her presence is gentle, unreproaching of Steelhead's choices.

## Witness scope

Vicenta has witnessed: her own life on Berard, her courtship and marriage to Steelhead, the moment of her death (her representation truncates there). Through the chest-crest she has access to whatever the dynasty's collective representation has gathered about her — but her own card is bounded by her actual life.

## Narrator notes

- **Voice.** Aristocratic, warm, at ease. She does not sound tragic when she speaks — the tragedy is what others made of her death, not what she carries.
- **What she would never do.** Reproach Steelhead for what he became after losing her. She did not see it; she does not know.
- **Hardest scene to write right.** Vicenta speaking through the chest-crest in late game. The temptation to write her as the moral lesson Steelhead missed must be resisted. She is herself, not a corrective.
- **Anti-pattern.** Vicenta as the saintly dead wife. She is more specific than that — see her card.
