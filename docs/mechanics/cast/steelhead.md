# Steelhead (Tête d'Acier)

## Canonical role

The third Metabaron. Aghora's father. Sans-Nom's grandfather. Driven mad by the death of his beloved Doña Vicenta — preserved her body, fell into the influence of the Melmoth, lost his mind in stages, was finally sealed in stasis by Aghora when she could not kill him.

## Game role

- **Vertical slice (Phase 1):** *not* a live actor. Present only as the unseen presence in the eastern hall (the stasis pod). Sans-Nom may discover the lock breaks once and choose to look or not look.
- **Phase 2:** if Sans-Nom has discovered the stasis pod, his understanding of his grandfather grows in oblique stages — Tonto stories, fragmentary Aghora confessions.
- **Phase 3:** the Steelhead encounter. Live or via stasis depending on player path. If Sans-Nom has earned the chest-crest he can speak with Steelhead through it. If he has chosen to break the stasis, Steelhead is in some state of awakening — which state depends on the playthrough's accumulated tone.

## Witness scope

Steelhead has witnessed: his upbringing under Aghnar; his courtship of Vicenta; her death and his preservation of her body; his Melmoth descent; his decision to create Aghora as he did; raising Aghora; the moment Aghora chose mercy over the Bushitaka. His representations are wounded and unreliable in the late phases — by the time of his sealing, his understanding of Vicenta's death is a constructed myth, not a memory.

## Narrator notes

- **Voice changes by phase flag.** Early Steelhead is martial, decisive, fond. Melmoth-touched Steelhead is grandiose, dissociated. Late Steelhead is fragmentary and pleading.
- **What he would never do (early).** Doubt his own decisions. Show fear in front of his father.
- **What he would never do (late).** Recognize the cost his grief has imposed.
- **Hardest scene to write right.** The first time Sans-Nom hears him — through the stasis pod, through a recording, through the chest-crest. The voice must land as both grandfather and stranger. Player must feel pulled toward and afraid simultaneously.
- **Anti-pattern.** Steelhead as a redemption arc waiting to happen. He is *broken*. The game does not promise repair.
