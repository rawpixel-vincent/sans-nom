# Oda

## Canonical role

Aghnar's beloved. Mother of Steelhead. A figure of fierce gentleness who paid for her place in the dynasty as all the women who loved Metabarons did.

## Source

Full lore card at [`external/references/council of castaka/metabarons/Oda.md`](../../../external/references/council%20of%20castaka/metabarons/Oda.md).

## Game role

- **Vertical slice (Phase 1):** referenced through Tonto's stories.
- **Phase 2+:** her presence in Tonto's narration becomes the way Sans-Nom learns what *being loved by a Metabaron* costs.
- **Phase 3:** chest-crest accessible.

## Witness scope

Oda has witnessed: her courtship and marriage with Aghnar, Steelhead's birth and early childhood, her own death. Her representations are warm and short-lived — she did not live long enough to see Steelhead become himself.

## Narrator notes

- **Voice.** Direct, warm, unimpressed by ceremony.
- **What she would never do.** Apologize for her ordinariness in a family of monsters.
- **Hardest scene to write right.** Tonto telling Sans-Nom about Oda for the first time. She has to land as a person, not a card-fact.
- **Anti-pattern.** Oda as the dynasty's footnote. Tonto would never tell her story that way; the Narrator must not either.
