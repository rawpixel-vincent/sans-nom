# IP Commitments

What canon forbids and why. The reference list the Narrator's [peer card](../../architecture/narrator-agent/peer-card.md) leans on, and the list designers consult when authoring scenes or generating NPCs.

These are not exhaustive. They are the load-bearing prohibitions — the things that, if violated, would break the IP's grammar.

## Hard prohibitions

### The Bushitaka

See [bushitaka.md](bushitaka.md). Cannot be casually broken. Cannot be reframed as merely cruel. Cannot be exempted by the system.

### Honorata's specificity

She is not the dynasty's dark mother / pure villain. The IP rejects this read; the Narrator must too. See [Honorata's cast file](../cast/honorata.md). Her tenderness is real, AND her control is real.

### The cost of love

Every union in the dynasty pays a price. Othon paid in castration. Steelhead paid in Vicenta's death and his subsequent madness. Aghora paid in her divergence from the Bushitaka. Generated NPCs and original sub-plots that involve love must carry a real cost; cheap romance is canon-violating.

### Witness-bound knowledge

[Pillar 2](../../principles/pillars/02-characters-witness-bound.md). No character ever knows something they shouldn't. Convenient revelations are forbidden.

### The Shabda-Oud

Honorata's order. Their powers (psychic conditioning, surgical genius) are bounded to the priestess line. Generated characters cannot possess these powers without canonical justification.

### The gravity of the dynasty

Sans-Nom does not get to escape his bloodline through wit or refusal. He may struggle; the world responds. But the dynasty's weight is real and inescapable. See [Pillar 1](../../principles/pillars/01-ip-is-law.md).

## Soft commitments (Narrator should hold these unless overruled by a specific scene)

### Tone of operatic excess

The saga is grand. Small scenes can be quiet, but the *frame* is operatic. Narration should reach for the cosmic register when the moment allows.

### Time compression

The Metabarons saga moves through generations. The game should too. Long [Wait](../verbs/wait.md) skips are encouraged; small-scale realism is not the goal.

### Names matter

Naming a character is a moment. *Sans-Nom* itself is a name about not having a name. The Narrator should treat naming events with weight.

### Death is not the end

Through Tonto's stories, recordings, the chest-crest, and apparitions, the dead participate. The architecture is built for this. See [dead-and-absent.md](../cast/dead-and-absent.md).

## Maintenance

This list should grow as Phase 0 playtest surfaces new patterns the Narrator is getting wrong. Each addition should name what was wrong and what the commitment is. New commitments require designer review.
